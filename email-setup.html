Set up your ACE email

Aviation Career Enrichment, Inc. — for staff and board members with a new @flyace.org address.

1. First sign-in (everyone)

  1. You received a welcome email at your personal address with your new ACE address, and a text message with a temporary password. Keep both.
  2. On any computer, go to outlook.office.com and sign in with your ACE address and the temporary password.
  3. Choose a new password when asked (at least 12 characters is best).
  4. When asked for more information, install the free Microsoft Authenticator app on your phone and follow the steps. It protects your account and the student and family information in it.

If the text never arrives or the password is rejected, email acestaff@flyace.org from your personal address and an administrator will send a new one.

2. Add your mailbox to your devices

Any computer — in your web browser

Go to outlook.office.com and sign in. Nothing to install. You can also open Word, Excel and PowerPoint in the browser from the waffle menu (top left).

The free ACE plan includes the web and phone versions of Office, not the desktop programs.

Windows

  1. Open the Outlook app (Windows 11 includes it; otherwise install "Outlook (new)" from the Microsoft Store).
  2. Choose Add account, enter your ACE address, and select Continue.
  3. Sign in with your password and approve the Authenticator prompt.

Mac

Easiest: use outlook.office.com in your browser, or install the free Microsoft Outlook app from the App Store.

Apple Mail:

  1. Open Mail → Mail ▸ Add Account…
  2. Choose Microsoft Exchange and select Continue.
  3. Enter your name and ACE address, choose Sign In, then Sign In again when asked to use Microsoft.
  4. Enter your password and approve the Authenticator prompt. Tick Mail (and Calendar/Contacts if you like).

iPhone, iPad and Android — Outlook app (recommended)

  1. Install Microsoft Outlook from the App Store or Google Play.
  2. Open it, tap Add Account, enter your ACE address and tap Continue.
  3. Sign in and approve the Authenticator prompt.

Built-in Mail app

iPhone: Settings → Mail → Accounts → Add Account → Microsoft Exchange → enter your ACE address and sign in.
Android: use the Outlook app above; it is more reliable than the built-in mail app.

Read ACE email from Gmail, Yahoo or another personal account

Google is ending Gmail's old "check mail from other accounts" feature in 2026, and Microsoft does not allow the older password-only connection it used. Use one of the options below instead.

Option A — Gmail app on your phone (ACE and personal mail in one place)

  1. Open the Gmail app → tap your profile picture → Add another account.
  2. Choose Outlook, Hotmail and Live (this also works for ACE's Microsoft accounts).
  3. Enter your ACE address, sign in, and approve the Authenticator prompt.

When you reply, check that the "From" address is your ACE address so families see the right sender.

Option B — Outlook app (works with any provider)

The Outlook app can hold your ACE account and your Gmail, Yahoo or other accounts together. Add ACE as in the Phone tab, then use Settings → Add account for your personal ones.

Option C — Forwarding to your personal inbox

An administrator can forward a copy of your ACE mail to your personal address. This works with any provider, but replies will come from your personal address unless you also add your ACE account as in Option A or B. Student and family information may then sit in an account ACE does not control, so ask only if you need it.

Yahoo Mail app

We have not confirmed that the Yahoo Mail app can connect to ACE's Microsoft accounts. Please use the Outlook app (Option B) with your Yahoo account added, or the web at outlook.office.com.

Common problems

  • "Incorrect password" on the first try: type the temporary password exactly as texted (no spaces) or ask for a reset.
  • Asked for a code you don't have: finish setting up the Authenticator app first (step 1).
  • Left your phone behind or got a new phone: contact an administrator to reset your sign-in.
  • Never share your password or Authenticator approvals — ACE staff will never ask for them.

Still stuck? Email acestaff@flyace.org from your personal address.
